# Proposal

## Why
Wireless denial-of-service and kick attacks (such as those generated by `aireplay-ng --deauth`, `mdk4 d`, ESP32 Marauder, and Flipper Zero) transmit bursts of spoofed 802.11 management frames to disconnect client stations from Access Points. Attack tools commonly broadcast **Disassociation frames (subtype `0xA0`)**, either exclusively or interleaved with Deauthentication frames (subtype `0xC0`), to knock clients offline or force 4-way handshake re-negotiation.

BeeWave currently inspects only subtype `0xC0` (`DEAUTH_FC0`), allowing pure disassociation floods to pass completely undetected. Detecting active wireless kick attacks requires monitoring and tracking 802.11 Disassociation frames (subtype `0xA0`) in the sniffer pipeline alongside deauthentication frames, alerting the operator when disassociation rates exceed the configured threshold.

## What Changes
- Add `DisassocAlert { bssid: [u8; 6], count: u16 }` variant to `TelemetryMessage` in `crates/protocol`.
- In `crates/core`, extend deauth/kick monitoring to track disassociation frame arrivals per BSSID over a 1-second sliding window without heap allocation, emitting `DisassocAlert` when thresholds are crossed.
- In `firmware`, recognize 802.11 subtype `0xA0` (`DISASSOC_FC0`) in the promiscuous sniffer callback and feed disassociation counts to the application task.
- Regenerate golden wire test vectors (`make vectors`) and mirror the decoder in `web/src/shared/protocol/`.
- In `web`, ingest `DisassocAlert` in `AlertStore` and display alerts in the alert feed (`[DISASSOC FLOOD] Disassociation flood from <BSSID> (<count>/s)`).
- Add mock disassociation flood generation in `MockWorld` and comprehensive unit tests across protocol, core, and web.

## Non-goals and Invariants
- BeeWave remains strictly **receive-only**; no frames are transmitted.
- Wire protocol changes are non-breaking additions appended to `TelemetryMessage`.
- Device core remains `#![no_std]` and allocation-free.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `device-core`: Track 802.11 Disassociation frames per BSSID over a 1-second window and emit `DisassocAlert` when the threshold is reached.
- `wire-protocol`: Define `DisassocAlert { bssid: [u8; 6], count: u16 }` on `TelemetryMessage` and update test vectors.
- `web-client`: Ingest and display `DisassocAlert` events in the alert feed and toast notifications.

## Impact
- `crates/protocol`: Appends `DisassocAlert` variant to `TelemetryMessage`.
- `crates/core`: Adds disassociation window evaluation in `features/deauth_detect/` and `app/monitoring.rs`.
- `firmware`: Updates promiscuous packet filter callback to count subtype `0xA0` frames.
- `web`: Updates TypeScript protocol decoder, alert store, and alert formatting.
